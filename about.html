About MediScan

Empowering early cancer detection with cutting-edge AI and medical imaging.

Who We Are

MediScan is a healthcare technology initiative focused on building intelligent and accessible cancer screening tools using artificial intelligence. We specialize in image-based diagnosis using YOLO models and deep neural networks. Our mission is to provide quick, accurate, and patient-friendly detection for prostate, liver, and lung cancers.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between early diagnosis and treatment through automated AI-powered systems that reduce diagnostic errors, save time, and support healthcare professionals in underserved regions.

Our Technology

  • YOLOv5 for object detection in histopathology images
  • CNN & RNN models for classifying cancer types
  • Secure Cloud Deployment for patient record access

Meet the Team

Our interdisciplinary team includes data scientists, pathologists, and software engineers from across India working together to modernize cancer diagnostics.

Why Early Detection Matters

Cancer survival rates increase dramatically with early diagnosis. Traditional biopsy reviews are time-consuming and prone to variation. OncoDetect leverages deep learning to improve diagnostic accuracy and reduce turnaround time from days to seconds.
